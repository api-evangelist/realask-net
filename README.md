Realask provides verified answers from the real world for AI agents. It operates in the United States, offering a verification service that ensures AI agents receive accurate, trustworthy information. The platform emphasizes privacy, with contact via hello@realask.net, and includes clear terms, privacy policy, and opt‑out options. Realask does not handle sales calls, marketing, or financial transactions directly, focusing solely on delivering reliable data to AI systems.

**Areas:** Company · AI · Verification · Data · Privacy

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Realask: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
